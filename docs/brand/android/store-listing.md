## Full description (4,000 characters at most)

<!-- full -->
Talk instead of typing, in any app. ThumbFree puts a small yellow bubble next to the text box you are typing in. Tap it, say what you want to write, and tap again. Your words appear where your cursor is: in messages, email, notes and search boxes.

Everything happens on your phone. ThumbFree turns speech into text without the internet, so your voice and your words never leave your device.

HOW IT WORKS
• Tap a text box in any app. The bubble appears beside it.
• Tap the bubble and speak. Tap again to stop, or hold the bubble while you talk and let go when you are done.
• Your words are typed into the box, with the spacing right.
• Setup takes three steps: download a speech model, allow the microphone, and turn on the bubble in Android's accessibility settings.

CHOOSE YOUR SPEECH MODEL
• English (recommended): the most accurate for English.
• Multilingual: 25 European languages, and it hears which one you speak by itself. Bulgarian, Croatian, Czech, Danish, Dutch, English, Estonian, Finnish, French, German, Greek, Hungarian, Italian, Latvian, Lithuanian, Maltese, Polish, Portuguese, Romanian, Russian, Slovak, Slovenian, Spanish, Swedish and Ukrainian.
• Switch in Settings whenever you like. A new choice applies from your next take.

PRIVATE AND OFFLINE
• Speech recognition runs on your phone. Your audio and text never leave it.
• The internet is used only to download the speech model you choose (English about 731 MB, Multilingual about 740 MB), over Wi-Fi unless you allow mobile data. After that ThumbFree works in airplane mode.
• No account, no sign-in and no ads. The one permission ThumbFree asks for is the microphone.

FAST
• With the English model, text appears about 0.7 to 0.9 seconds after you stop talking, whether you spoke for 4 seconds or 30 (measured on a Pixel 10).
• Long takes are transcribed in pieces while you are still talking, so there is little left to do when you stop.

ACCURATE
• The English model is NVIDIA's Parakeet, with a 3.5% word error rate on our public English test clips.
• Dictionary: add the names and terms you say often (family, friends, places, work terms). ThumbFree writes them your way, and with the English model it also fixes words it heard almost right.

A BUBBLE THAT STAYS OUT OF YOUR WAY
• The bubble shows only while you are typing in a text box, and hides everywhere else.
• Drag it anywhere on the screen and it stays there. Turn on Snap to screen edge if you like it at the side.
• Choose its size, and how transparent it is while it waits.

YOUR HISTORY, YOUR RULES
• Every take is kept on your phone: search it, copy it or transcribe it again.
• By default takes are kept for 7 days, and at most 200 of them. Choose 7, 30 or 90 days or forever, and at most 50, 200 or 1,000 takes or no limit.
• Clear all your takes whenever you like.

WHY THUMBFREE USES ACCESSIBILITY
ThumbFree uses Android's accessibility service to show the bubble next to the text box you are in, and to type your words into that box after you tap. It reads only the focused text box (where the cursor is and the text around it) so your words and spacing land in the right place. It does not read anything else on your screen, it never types into password fields, and nothing it reads leaves your phone. The microphone is used only while you record, and Android shows its microphone indicator while it is on.

GOOD TO KNOW
• Needs Android 13 or newer, and about 1.8 GB of free space for the model download.
• Built on open-source speech tools: transcribe.cpp and ggml (MIT), Silero VAD (MIT) and NVIDIA's Parakeet models (the English one under the NVIDIA Open Model License, the Multilingual one under CC BY 4.0). The credits are in Settings > About.
