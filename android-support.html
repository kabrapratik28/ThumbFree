ThumbFree for Android: help

ThumbFree puts a small yellow bubble next to the text field you are typing in. Tap it, speak, and your words are typed in, in any app. It works on your phone, without the internet. It needs Android 13 or newer.

Set it up

1. Open ThumbFree and tap Get started. It downloads the English speech model (about 731 MB) over Wi-Fi; tap "Other language?" first if you want the Multilingual model. 2. Allow the microphone and choose "While using the app". 3. Turn on the bubble. Tap Agree and open settings, then tap ThumbFree, turn on "Use ThumbFree" (leave "ThumbFree shortcut" off), and tap Allow when Android asks.

Use it

  • In any app, tap a text field. The yellow bubble appears next to it.
  • Tap the bubble and speak. It shows a red ring while it listens. Tap it again to stop, and your words appear where the cursor is. Or hold the bubble while you talk and let go to finish.
  • Drag the bubble anywhere; it stays where you drop it. Change its size and transparency in Settings > Bubble.

Questions

The bubble doesn't show. It appears only while a text field has focus. Check that "Use ThumbFree" is on in Android's settings (Accessibility > ThumbFree). ThumbFree never types into password fields.

It stopped working after I force-stopped it or updated my phone. Android turns an accessibility service off when its app is force-stopped. Open Accessibility > ThumbFree and turn "Use ThumbFree" on again.

It asks for the microphone again. If you chose "Only this time", Android takes the permission back later. Open ThumbFree, tap Allow and choose "While using the app".

Does it work offline? Yes. Once the speech model is on your phone, ThumbFree needs no internet, even in airplane mode.

Which languages? The English model is the most accurate for English and is recommended. The Multilingual model understands 25 European languages; switch in Settings > Speech model.

It spells a name wrong. Add it in the Dictionary tab, and ThumbFree spells it your way from then on.

Where are my recordings? Only on your phone, in History. They are never uploaded and not included in backups. By default ThumbFree keeps takes for 7 days and at most 200 takes; change this in Settings > History.

How much space does it need? About 731 MB for the English model, plus a little free space while it downloads.
